Product range

Bearing types in the catalogue

SKF and FAG ball bearings from 10 mm to 100 mm bore. Each type suits a different combination of load, speed and alignment.

Deep Groove

  • Series 60, 62, 63, 160
  • Radial + moderate axial loads
  • High speeds, low noise
  • Open, 2Z / 2ZR shields, 2RS1 / 2RSR seals

Angular Contact · Single Row

  • Series 72B, 73B
  • 40° contact angle
  • High axial load, one direction
  • BEP: glass-fibre polyamide cage

Angular Contact · Double Row

  • Series 32A, 33A
  • 30° contact angle
  • Axial loads in both directions
  • Compact replacement for a pair

Self-Aligning

  • Series 12, 13, 22, 23
  • Tolerates shaft misalignment
  • Cylindrical or tapered (K) bore
  • Low friction, moderate loads

Design data

Theory & formulas

Rolling contact (anti-friction) bearings use balls or rollers between surfaces in relative motion. Selection balances load capacity, speed, life, alignment and space.

Equivalent dynamic load

P = X·V·Fᵣ + Y·Fₐ

V = race-rotation factor, X = radial factor, Y = thrust factor. X and Y depend on bearing type and Fₐ/Fᵣ compared with e.

Load–life equation

L₁₀ = (C / P)p

L₁₀ in million revolutions; p = 3 for ball bearings, 10/3 for roller bearings.

Life in hours

L₁₀ = 60·n·L₁₀ₕ / 10⁶

n = speed (rpm), L₁₀ₕ = rated life (hours).

Cyclic loads

Pₑ = [ΣNP³ / ΣN]1/3

Equivalent load for a work cycle; continuous variation: Pₑ = [(1/N)∫P³dN]1/3.

Reliability

L/L₁₀ = [ln(1/R) / ln(1/R₉₀)]1/b

Weibull distribution with a = 6.84, b = 1.17. System: RS = RN.

System life

L = f(Lr, Lre, Lc, Ll, Ls)

Raceways, rolling elements, cage, lubricant and seals – the weakest component limits the bearing life.
